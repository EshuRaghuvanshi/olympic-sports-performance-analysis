Strategist: Ms. Priya • NOC High Performance Directorate

Strategic Scenario Objective

Ms. Priya is responsible for optimizing athlete quota allocations ahead of the upcoming Olympic cycle across 50 sports and 60 nations. By analyzing athlete accreditation records against podium finishes, Priya isolates disciplines that generate the highest per-athlete medal return — discovering that Rowing generates 6.916 medals per athlete and Shooting generates 6.731 medals per athlete. Comparing this against superpower baselines led by the United States (735 total medals) and China (696 total medals) allows Priya to reallocate capital toward high-yield, compact squads.
